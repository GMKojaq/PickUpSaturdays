One day. New adventures.

Pick-up & Drop-in Saturdays is a monthly online event for tabletop roleplaying games. Volunteer GMs bring games to the table, and players choose the sessions that sound fun. Come to play, run a game, or try something new.

When: Planned for the last Saturday of each month.
Where: Online. Session links and platform details will be shared with registered players.
Next event: Date, times, and sign-ups will be posted here when confirmed.

Each listing will tell you what game is being played, what to expect, and how to claim a seat. New players are welcome; check the individual listing for any preparation needed.

How to join

  1. Browse the lineup. Read the system, time zone, player count, experience level, and content notes.
  2. Choose a game. Use the registration link in its listing once sign-ups open.
  3. Get ready to play. Your GM will share the online table details and anything you need before the session.

Times and sign-up details are still being arranged. Check this page for confirmed information before making plans.

Bring a game to the table

Want to GM? One-shots and short, welcoming sessions work especially well for a pick-up day. When submitting a game, prepare a title, a two- or three-sentence pitch, the system, start time and time zone, expected length, seat count, player experience needed, and any content notes.
